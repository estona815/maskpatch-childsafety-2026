How it works

A tiny local token classifier suggests name, school and address spans. Separate pattern rules suggest email and phone-shaped text. Review each source span, choose changes, and keep the original in memory for restoration. Copy happens only when you press the button.

AI provenance: Codex wrote the application, synthetic training examples and training code. This prototype makes no claim about a participant’s unaided work or experience.

This optional download includes the original draft. Keep it private.

Limits

English synthetic examples only. Names and details can be missed, and ordinary words can be flagged. Model scores are uncalibrated, not probabilities that a suggestion is right. A quiet result never means a prompt is safe to share.

The 64-sentence entity-disjoint synthetic holdout shares training grammar. A separate four-sentence challenge has 1 false positive and 4 missed entity tokens out of 7. These small artificial tests are not real-world accuracy evidence.

No cloud inference, analytics, automatic saving or child data collection. Text stays in this page’s memory. Do not use real children’s personal information in the demo. Closing or reloading clears the draft.
